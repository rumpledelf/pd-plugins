# Hourglass

Tap the hourglass, or use Space / Enter, to turn it. Sand takes 30 seconds to drain fully (configurable with `seconds`, 5–300). Turning partway through reverses the amount of sand remaining. Repeated input during the turn is ignored. Reduced motion skips rotation and the falling stream but retains moving sand levels.

Sand levels use the bulb outline's integrated volume, so transfer remains consistent through its changing width.
