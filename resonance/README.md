# Resonance

Press Play and sweep the pitch. Near resonance the glass wobbles more; hold
steady for a few seconds and the bowl breaks into falling pieces with a quiet
synthesized tink. Reset restores the glass; its resonant frequency stays at 535 Hz.

535 Hz is based on Vanderbilt's reported 533–540 Hz range for particular Libbey
white-wine glasses, not a universal glass-breaking frequency. Real glass resonance
varies, and breakage also requires sufficiently intense sound:
https://www.vanderbilt.edu/physicsdemonstration/davesdemos/demonstrations/demo200.htm

A stylized demonstration, not a sound-pressure simulation. Browser audio stays
at a modest fixed gain. Visible vibration is slowed for readability. Reduced
motion omits wobbling and drops fragments straight to rest. Audio stops when
the page is hidden or reset.
