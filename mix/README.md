# Mix

Hold the stick (pointer, touch, Space or Enter) to stir layered colours into ribbons and a blended mixture. Release to stop; motion settles over less than half a second. Reset restores separate layers.

Self-contained canvas advection and diffusion of a colour field, clipped to a wide tapered glass. No dependencies. 250px total; keyboard focus highlights the stick. Reduced motion reduces stick travel and flow strength.
