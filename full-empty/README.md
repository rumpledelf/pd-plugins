# Full and empty

Tap an apple on the moving rail to drop it straight down. Apples fully over the
bucket opening are caught; misses fall out of the frame without a penalty.
Twelve apples fill the bucket. Rail speed increases with each catch and stops
when full. Reset empties the bucket and restores the starting speed.

Touch/click and Enter/Space work. Flat host-coloured artwork, fixed footer,
no focus effects or shine. Essential movement is retained for this timing game.
Physics is substepped and paused in a hidden tab. No external dependencies.

Test: `node --test full-empty/full-empty.test.cjs`
Review: http://localhost:8080/plugin-tester.html?plugin=full-empty

Manifest page is a suggested word target, not a host/database change.
