# Russian doll

Click the smallest visible doll (or focus the activity and press Enter/Space) to open its cap, extract a smaller doll, and let it hop beside its parent. Each click reveals exactly one doll. Once all five are out, click to nest them together again. The five sizes, shell colours and flowers match Sort.

Adapted from the supplied russian-dolls-loader-clearance.html artwork and motion, recoloured to the documented host palette. Fixed 250px activity, no libraries, no auto-loop; reduced motion shortens each movement.
