# Towers of Hanoi

Move the four-disc tower from left to right. Drag a top disc, or select its peg and then its destination. Larger discs cannot land on smaller discs; invalid drops return. Fifteen moves is optimal.

Keyboard: Tab focuses a peg; Left/Right changes pegs; Enter/Space selects source or destination; Escape cancels selection. Reset is always available, including during movement. Reduced motion snaps discs into position.
