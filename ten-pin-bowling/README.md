# 10 Pin Bowling

A compact two-ball bowling frame based on the quoits interaction. Drag the ball
left or right to choose a starting position. Drag elsewhere on the lane to set
the angle; that gesture cannot also throw. Press and hold on the lane again to
build power, then release to bowl.

Touch, mouse and pen use the whole lane as the target. Keyboard users can move
the ball with Left and Right, adjust the angle with Shift+Left and Shift+Right,
hold Space or Enter to charge, and release to bowl. Escape cancels a gesture.

Test through
`http://localhost:8080/plugin-tester.html?plugin=ten-pin-bowling` after starting
`python3 -m http.server 8080` from the repository root.
