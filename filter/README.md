# Filter

Hold the jug (pointer, Space or Enter) to pour water containing green particles through a paper cone. Clear water drips into a beaker, while particles remain in the filter. This illustrates suspended particles, not removing dissolved colour.

The jug's horizontal surface is calculated from rotated vessel area. Release stops pouring; remaining water drains through. Reset restores the source. Falling drops borrow the hourglass dash and landing-fleck animation, locally adapted without a runtime dependency. Simple black outlines, host green and faint clear-water shading. Reduced motion keeps drops static.

Test: `http://localhost:8080/plugin-tester.html?plugin=filter`.
