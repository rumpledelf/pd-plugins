# Fill, drain and overflow

Turn either tap on/off, or pull/replace the purple plug by tapping it.
Both taps together fill the bath in about seven seconds; one takes about
fourteen. With the plug out, both taps very slowly fill the bath (about 100
seconds from empty); one tap or neither lets it drain. At capacity, any excess
water spills over the two ends.
Reset shuts both taps, replaces the plug and empties the bath.

The side-on bath is a cutaway so its water level and plug remain visible.
Touch/click and Enter/Space work. Reduced motion removes the tap/plug
transitions but retains water motion that demonstrates the activity.
No dependencies, audio, decorative shine or plugin focus effects.

Test: `node --test fill-drain/fill-drain.test.cjs`
