# Chords

Choose a root and major/minor quality, then Play chord. Three keys depress and sound simultaneously. Minor lowers the third one semitone. Magenta marks show the selected chord before playing. Tap individual keys to hear notes.

Flat two-octave keyboard, explicit user-triggered Web Audio only. Triangle oscillators use a short attack/release envelope and low master gain. Changing selection, hiding the page or removing the plugin stops sound and closes its audio context. Native labelled controls and key buttons support keyboard/touch.
